01 — About
A career built across culture.

Harry Dingwall is a British creative, entrepreneur and multi-disciplinary artist from Derbyshire whose work spans fashion, cinema, music and business.

Beginning in fashion and modelling, Harry went on to build a career across international fashion, film, music and entrepreneurship.

His work combines creative direction, design, performance and business with a belief in independence, ambition and pushing established boundaries.
